How to Play

The deal

Seven cards go to each of seven columns. In the first four columns the top three cards are face down; every other card is face up. The last three cards are set aside face down as the reserve, in the top-left corner.

Moving cards

  • Drag a face-up card onto the last card of another column. Every card below it moves too, even if the card is buried in the middle of its column.
  • Which cards may go on which depends on the difficulty level: Easy, Medium or Difficult. Nothing can go on an Ace.
  • Only a King, with any cards below it, can move into an empty column.
  • When a move uncovers a face-down card, it turns over.
  • Click the reserve to deal its three cards onto the first three columns. You can do this once, at any time.

Winning

You win when every card is sorted into columns running King to Ace in one suit. The other three columns are then empty.
