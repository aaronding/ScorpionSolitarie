Scorpion Solitaire

Scorpion is a one-deck solitaire. The goal is to sort the whole deck into four columns, each running from King down to Ace in a single suit.

Unlike most solitaires there are no foundation piles: everything happens in the seven columns on the table. Any face-up card can be moved, together with every card below it, which makes Scorpion a game of planning more than luck.

Every game you are dealt can be won. Before dealing, the computer shuffles until it finds a deal it can solve.
