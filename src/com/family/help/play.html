Using the Game

  • Drag a face-up card to move it and the cards below it. Drop it anywhere over the column you want.
  • Double-click a card to send it to the best place: onto the next card up in its own suit if possible, otherwise into an empty column (Kings), otherwise anywhere it fits.
  • Hold the right mouse button (or Control-click on a Mac) on a partly covered card to see all of it.
  • Hint (press H) highlights a move you could make. Press it again for another.
  • Undo and Redo work as far as the difficulty level allows.
  • Game numbers: every deal has a number, shown at the bottom of the window. Use Select Game Number to play a particular deal, and Restart This Game to try the same deal again.
  • Settings (in the Scorpion Solitaire menu on a Mac, the Game menu elsewhere) sets the difficulty for your next game and the design on the back of the cards, which can be any picture of your own.
  • The game in progress is saved automatically when you quit, and continues next time. Save and Load keep one extra game in Scorpion.dat in your home folder.
  • Statistics shows games played and won at each level.
